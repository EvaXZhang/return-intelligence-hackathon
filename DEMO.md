# Demo Script (v2)

Launch Return Intelligence on port 8504 as described in `README.md` (pin `DEMO_SEASON=WINTER`). Open the Customer Portal and the Staff Dashboard in separate browser tabs, then run **Reset synthetic demo → Reset and reseed** before rehearsing. Every step works in LIVE AI mode and in simulated mode; the button labels in brackets are the simulated ones.

1. **Reason and exchange-first solution.** Choose `SYN-O-SIZE-001` and enter "The shoes are too small and tight; I want a larger size". Click **Ask Bedrock agents to classify and recommend** [**Classify reason and show options**]. The structured reason is `Wrong Size / too_small` in both modes. "Exchange for an in-stock pair (recommended)" is preselected with size 9, and refund remains a choice. Open **Sent to your email (simulated)** to show the mock email to `customer-syn-o-size-001@example.test`; nothing is actually sent. Select the size-9 exchange and click **Save decision**.
2. **Early warning, status and flag.** In the Staff Dashboard the complaint joins four seeded ones. Show the **SKU + size** alert for `SYN-RUNNER-100|8`: 5/25 (20%), baseline 1.0%, lift 20×, window 2025-10-17 → 2026-01-15, and "not a confirmed defect". **Product / series flags** lists `SYN-MODEL-RUNNER`, and the request detail warns that its series is flagged. Click **Start investigation** (status INVESTIGATING, flag stays), and later **Close alert**: the flag disappears, Active suspected-pattern alerts drops to 0, and the alert moves to the collapsed **Closed alerts** list. Explain the second level: a series alert (`model_id`, all sizes) fires when every size shows the same problem, compared with other models in the same similar group.
3. **Photos and the staff confidence override.** Back in the Customer Portal, upload Front, Left and Right photos and click **Analyze 3 photos with Amazon Bedrock** [pick a **Synthetic photo scenario**, for example **Clean unworn pair**, and click **Submit 3 synthetic photos**]. The scenario names describe only the photos (Clean unworn pair, Minor handling marks, Clean pair with slight glare on one photo, Visible wear, Light try-on creasing, Sole edge separating, Small gap near the sole edge, Blurry or incomplete photos), so the customer never chooses or sees a confidence or the review path. In **Staff controls**, set **Demo photo-confidence override (staff only)** to Force 95% (auto-accept) or Force 75% (staff review). Note the caption: LLM self-reported confidence is not statistically calibrated.
4. **Retake.** Use blurry or incomplete photos [scenario **Blurry or incomplete photos**]. The AI asks for specific retakes and assigns no eligibility or grade.
5. **Review below 0.85, and the defect hold.** With the override on Use AI confidence, a worn result at 80% (`SYN-O-WORN-001`, "I wore these outside", refund, scenario **Light try-on creasing**) goes to **Human photo review**; **Confirm AI: WORN_INELIGIBLE** closes it, and worn shoes are never Grade C. For `SYN-O-DEFECT-001` ("The sole is separating, quality issue", scenario **Sole edge separating**) the request reaches the **Suspected-defect hold**. Resolve it as Not a defect (pick a grade, back to the normal flow) or Confirmed defect (replacement released or refund recorded, `DEFECT_RESOLVED`, never routed or resold).
6. **Return method and the staff final destination.** Use `SYN-O-B-001` ("I prefer another style", refund, scenario **Minor handling marks**, then **Confirm AI** in review for Grade B). The customer sees **Drop off at a nearby store · FREE (recommended)** first and **Ship with a prepaid label · $9** second. The $9 applies to every mail-in, exchanges included. After **Confirm return method** the customer sees only the return store. In the Staff Dashboard, **Final destination (staff decision)** shows the system suggestion (an outlet for Grade B) as pending. Save it as is, or pick another destination with a reason.
7. **Exchange completion.** For the step-1 exchange, after photos and the return method, click **Generate return label + exchange confirmation**, then **Simulate original return received → release replacement**. The request reaches `COMPLETED_EXCHANGE` and shows the released size-9 replacement.
8. **Missing item and out of stock.** `SYN-O-MISSING-001` ("My item never arrived and is missing") offers **Reship the missing item** or a refund, with no photos or label. `SYN-O-OOS-001` ("The shoes were out of stock and never shipped") offers **Ship a similar in-stock style instead** in the same size, or a refund.
9. **KPIs and activity timeline.** The second metric row shows Exchanges, Refunds, Keeps, Exchange rate, Retention rate ((exchanges + keeps) ÷ decided) and Store drop-off share; "—" means no data yet. Expand the **Multi-Agent activity** events to show the public rationale and provider details, never chain-of-thought.

For an offline run without model calls, launch with `AI_BACKEND=simulated AWS_INTEGRATION_ENABLED=false DEPLOYMENT_ENABLED=false` on port 8504. The eight condition × seasonality route cells are tested exactly in both modes because AI cannot override them.
